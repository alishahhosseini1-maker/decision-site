Alex Morrison
Chief Revenue Officer · Salesforce
Decision
Should I pitch Alex Morrison at Salesforce on our enterprise platform given his CRO role and that we connected 8 days ago?
Context
Alex owns the revenue stack decision at Salesforce — a company that both buys enterprise platforms and serves as a reference architecture for how enterprise sales organizations should operate, making him simultaneously a potential buyer and a market signal. Salesforce is mid-cycle on several internal platform evaluations as it integrates its Slack and MuleSoft acquisitions, which means new tools that complement rather than compete with existing investments are more likely to get a hearing right now. What I bring is a platform that reduces friction in the exact part of the revenue process that CROs lose sleep over — which maps directly to Alex's accountability. The decision hinges entirely on whether Alex is currently in evaluation mode or locked into a vendor freeze for the fiscal year — these require completely different opening approaches. What I don't know is his current vendor commitment calendar or whether he has a platform equivalent already deployed across his team. Pitching a locked-in CRO who can't act for 9 months wastes the best connection window I have with someone at his seniority.
Lisa Nakamura
Managing Director Digital Transformation · Accenture
Decision
Should I approach Lisa Nakamura at Accenture about a partnership or referral arrangement rather than a direct sale, given her Managing Director role in digital transformation?
Context
Lisa deploys enterprise platforms at Fortune 500 clients for a living — which means she's not a buyer, she's a multiplier, and treating her like a buyer would be a fundamental misread of the relationship. Accenture's digital transformation practice is actively building its platform partner ecosystem, which creates a legitimate business reason for Lisa to add our platform to her recommendation set if the timing and client fit are right. What I offer her is a tool that makes her implementations faster and stickier — a genuine value add to her practice, not a sales pitch. The assumption this decision rests on is whether our platform fits the profile of tools Accenture currently recommends — if we're in a category they've already decided to own internally, partnership is unlikely. Unknown is her current platform partner list and whether our category is open or closed in her practice. Approaching Lisa with a sales pitch rather than a partnership conversation would immediately mark me as someone who doesn't understand how consulting firms work.
