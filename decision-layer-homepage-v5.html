Sample Verdict — Career Decision

Should I leave my job to join this startup as employee #4?

Software engineer, 8 years in. Series A just closed. Offer: 1.2% equity, 20% pay cut. Deadline: 2 weeks.

The Door — What you're actually deciding

You're not deciding whether this startup will succeed. You're deciding whether the identity shift — from established engineer to early employee — is something you want to have tried, regardless of outcome.

"The real risk isn't that the startup fails. It's that it succeeds moderately — and you spend 4 years in a role that's grown past what you were hired to build."

The Hinge — What everything is hanging on

Whether the founders will let you own the thing they hired you to build, or absorb you into execution once the product ships. This is a conversation you haven't had yet.

The Trap — What's waiting on the other side

You haven't asked what happens to your equity in a down round. Anti-dilution clauses matter more than the percentage. This needs a lawyer before the deadline — not after you've signed.

What to say to them — included in full verdict
"I want to own the thing you hired me to build. Can we put that in writing before I sign?"
Walk away if — included in full verdict
They can't answer what your role looks like 12 months after the product ships.
